## Part 01: Kickstarting Your Research Journey: Skills, Evidence, and Direction in the AI Age

Speaker: **Jiayi Zhang 张珈译**

A MAM UG student at University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC).
Now is a research assistant in Embodied AI at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University. Previously, worked as a research assistant in the IWIN-FINS Lab at SJTU and the IoT Lab at HKU.

Personal Website: <https://zhangjiayi24.github.io>

Email: [smyjz19@nottingham.edu.cn](mailto:smyjz19@nottingham.edu.cn)

### Brief

In this talk, I will discuss several key aspects of getting started in research and establishing a stable growth path:

1. What is Research
2. How to Start
3. Is It Necessary to Be Vertical to Your Own Field?
4. Opportunities and Platforms
5. Long-Term Consistency Practices
6. Q&A session
